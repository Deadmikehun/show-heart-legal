Show Heart Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Show Heart is designed to be private by design. Your supports, imported images, cards, and designs are stored locally on your device.

Data collection

Show Heart does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. The app does not use analytics, advertising trackers, user accounts, or cloud synchronization.

Your creations

The people, organizations, causes, places, or other things you choose to support are not transmitted to the developer. Images and other content you import into Show Heart remain on your device while being used by the app.

Sharing and exporting

Show Heart only sends a copy of a card outside the app when you explicitly choose to export or share it. If you share or export content to Photos, Files, social media, messaging applications, or other third-party services, those services may process the exported content according to their own privacy policies.

Local storage

Show Heart stores its library and designs locally on your device. The app does not provide cloud synchronization or an online backup service. Uninstalling the app, deleting its local data, or losing the device may result in the loss of locally stored designs.

Imported images

Show Heart may create local working copies of images you choose to import. These copies are used solely to create and edit your designs and are not uploaded to the developer.

Notifications

If you enable milestone reminders, Show Heart uses local notifications scheduled on your device. Notification scheduling does not require your support information to be sent to the developer.

Children's privacy

Show Heart does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users because the app does not collect personal data.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Show Heart's functionality changes. The current version will always be available at this page.
