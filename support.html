Show Heart Support

Show Heart is a private, offline creative app for making and sharing personalized cards for the people, places, organizations and causes that matter to you.

Need help?

Your data

Show Heart does not use accounts, analytics or cloud synchronization. Your library and designs are stored locally on your device.

Because Show Heart does not provide cloud backup, uninstalling the app, deleting its local data or losing your device may result in the loss of your locally stored designs.
